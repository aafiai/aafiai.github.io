Hi, I'm Aafia.

I'm a data and operations analyst based in Dubai, with 3+ years of experience turning messy operational data into something actually useful — clean dashboards, reliable reports, and insights that help teams make better decisions.

I hold a Bachelor's in Computer Science from BITS Pilani Dubai Campus, and have spent most of my career since at GE Vernova, managing reporting across MENA operations, building KPI dashboards, and keeping cross-functional teams aligned on what's happening on the ground. Before that I worked across pharma (MSD) and FMCG (HungerStation), so I've had to adapt quickly to very different data environments.

I'm good at Excel and VBA, comfortable owning reporting processes end to end, and genuinely enjoy the problem-solving side of analytics — figuring out why the numbers don't match, or building a tracker that saves someone an hour every week.

Currently exploring analyst opportunities in Dubai — feel free to reach out.
